Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Everything stays on your iPhone.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone? More → Settings → Your data → make a backup and save the file to Files or iCloud Drive. On the new phone, install Headroom and restore the backup from the same screen before logging anything, using the same passphrase.

How do I restore or manage a membership? More → Settings → Membership → Restore purchases or Manage subscription. You can also cancel in your Apple ID settings.

Apple Health isn't syncing. Open the Health app → your profile → Apps → Headroom and check the permissions.

Connecting a Garmin watch. Install Garmin Connect and the Headroom app from the Connect IQ Store on your watch, then in Headroom open More → Devices.
