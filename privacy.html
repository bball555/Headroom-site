Headroom Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-10-02

Headroom is a training planner for iPhone and Apple Watch. It is built so that your information stays on your devices. We don't run a server for the app, there is no account to create, and we don't collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter or that Headroom works out (your profile, goals, workouts, check-ins, soreness and pain flags, bodyweight, nutrition budget, progress photos and plan history) is stored only on your iPhone, and the parts the Watch needs are copied to your paired Apple Watch. Progress photos are stored encrypted inside the app. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Headroom reads workouts, heart rate, sleep, steps, bodyweight and nutrition from Apple Health so your plan reflects all your activity and recovery, and saves your sessions, bodyweight and nutrition back to Apple Health. Health data is used only to plan and log your training inside the app. It is never sent to us or anyone else, never used for advertising, and never stored in iCloud by Headroom. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app.

Other permissions

  • Calendar (optional): reads event dates and titles on your iPhone to suggest time away from training. Nothing leaves your device and nothing is added to your plan until you confirm it.
  • Camera (optional): takes progress photos, kept encrypted in the app and never uploaded.
  • Location (Apple Watch, during outdoor runs): records route, distance and pace for that run. It stays on your devices and in Apple Health if you allow it.
  • Bluetooth (optional): connects to your Garmin watch through Garmin's Connect IQ software to send your plan and bring back your workouts.
  • Notifications (optional): reminders created on your device. You choose how many a week.

Garmin watches

If you connect a Garmin watch, Headroom sends your sessions to it and receives your completed workouts back through the Garmin Connect app on your phone. Garmin's handling of data in Garmin Connect is covered by Garmin's privacy policy. Headroom does not send this data anywhere else.

Purchases

Memberships are bought through Apple. We don't see your payment details; the app only learns from Apple which membership is active on your Apple ID.

Exports and backups

You can export your data or make a backup at any time. A backup is a single file locked with a passphrase you choose, saved wherever you pick (Files, iCloud Drive, a computer). We can't open it and can't recover a lost passphrase.

No tracking, ads or analytics

Headroom contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking tools, and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Age

This version of Headroom is for people 18 and older. Onboarding asks for your birth year and does not continue for younger users.

Medical disclaimer

Headroom is a general wellness app. It is not a medical device, does not give medical advice, and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Changes to this policy

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example optional cloud sync), we will update this page and describe the change in the app before it applies to you.
